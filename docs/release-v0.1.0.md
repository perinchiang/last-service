## This version

A code-generated maintenance hall, a skippable awakening, mechanical arms, moving ventilation fans, industrial lighting and a restrained floor reflection. One pulse rifle with procedural mechanical hands and synthesized sound. Explore the room, inspect the access terminal, shoot the red interlock, survive two waves of security drones and operate the terminal to open the exit. The suspended cargo can be released by shooting its glowing latch.

## Controls

Desktop: WASD / arrows to move, mouse to look, left click to fire, right click to aim, E to interact, R to reload, Shift to move faster, C to lower the body, Esc to pause. If pointer lock is unavailable, dragging the scene also turns the view.

Phone: left pad to move, drag the right side to look, use the fire / interact / reload buttons. Landscape is recommended. Mobile controls are intended for convenient playtesting.

Automatic repair begins after five seconds without damage. Capacitors are unlimited but magazines require reloading. The HUD always shows the current objective. Use the pause or result menu to restart.

The scene is an original prototype. It does not reproduce another game's models, textures, sounds or story. Three.js is bundled with its MIT license notice.
